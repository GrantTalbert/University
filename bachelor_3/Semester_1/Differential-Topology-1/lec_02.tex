\lecture{2}{Fri 05 Sep 2025 12:20}{Smooth manifolds}

Given $f : M \to \mathbb{R} $ and a point $p$, we might want to differentiate $f$ at $p$. We can take an open neighborhood $p\in U$ and a homeomorphism $\varphi : U \cong \widetilde{U} \subseteq \mathbb{R} ^n$, and try to differentiate $f\circ \varphi ^{-1} $. The issue is that given another open set $V$ and another homeomorphism $\psi $, the change of variables from $f\circ \varphi ^{-1} $ to $f\circ \psi ^{-1} $ might not be smooth.
