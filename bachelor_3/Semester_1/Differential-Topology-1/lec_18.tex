\lecture{18}{Wed 15 Oct 2025 12:44}{Vector fields}

Recall that given a manifold $M$, a smooth vector field is a section $V : M \to TM$ of the projection of the tangent bundle $\pi : TM \to M$. The field $V$ at a point $p\in M$ then takes the form
\[
	V_p = V_p ^i \partial _i|_p
.\]
We can then define vector field in ways like
\[
	x\mapsto x^i \partial _i|_{x} 
.\]
If we choose a different chart on $M$, we could obtain a different coordinate function $\partial _i$ and get new fields.
