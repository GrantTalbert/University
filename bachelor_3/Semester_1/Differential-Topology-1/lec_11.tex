	Let $F : M \to N$ smooth. Then $F$ is a smooth immersion if and only if every $p\in M$ has a neighborhood $U\subseteq M$ such that $F|U$ is a smooth embedding.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Local section theorem]\label{???}
	Let $\pi : M \to N$ be smooth. Then $\pi $ is a smooth submersion if and only if every $p\in M$ is in the image of a smooth local section of $\pi $.
\end{theorem}

Smooth submersions are open, and if they are surjective then they are quotients.
