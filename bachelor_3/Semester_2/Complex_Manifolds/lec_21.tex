Now we want to look for global topological data on compact K\"ahler manifolds. All holomorphic $p$-forms, aka $(p,0)$-forms, are closed on K\"ahler manifolds. It is even true that the cohomology class of any nonzero holomorphic $p$-form is nonzero. What this means is that there are not a lot of holomorphic forms on compact K\"ahler manifolds.
