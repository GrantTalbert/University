We are discussing the exponential $\exp : \mathfrak{g}  \to G$ which relates the Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g} =T_eG$ to the Lie group $G$. We define $\exp (X)$ by integrating the left invariant vector field $X$.

Consider the exponential on Riemannian manifolds. There is a local geodesic so take a geodesic through $p$ with $X$ tangent to the geodesic. Then $\exp (X)$ is the point on the geodesic at time 1.

\begin{definition}\label{1.2.8}
	Let $\mathfrak{g} $ a Lie algebra. Then $\mathfrak{h} \subseteq \mathfrak{g} $ is a \emph{Lie subalgebra} if it is a linear subspace closed under multiplication. Equivalently, $\mathfrak{h} $ is a Lie algebra and $\mathfrak{h} \hookrightarrow \mathfrak{g} $ is a Lie algebra homomorphism.
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}\label{1.2.9}
	A Lie subgroup is a subgroup which is the image of an injective immersion into $G$.
\end{definition}
