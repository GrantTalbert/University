\chapter{Linear Algebra}
Linear algebra is the study of vector spaces and linear maps, or more generally, the category of $R$-modules for $R$ a field, often $\mathbb{R} $ or $\mathbb{C} $. However, much of the theory can be worked out over more general rings. In this chapter, $R$ will denote an integral domain, although much of what we do can be extended further to commutative rings.
