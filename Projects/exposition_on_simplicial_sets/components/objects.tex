\section{Simplicial objects}\label{sec5}

We offer a short digression from the main topic of this paper to give an introduction to simplicial objects in arbitrary categories. In some circumstances, simplicial objects in arbitrary categories $\C$ admit geometric realizations in $\C$, which differ from the geometric realization of simplicial sets. We introduce those, along with the corresponding dual notion of the totalization of cosimplicial sets and cosimplicial objects.
