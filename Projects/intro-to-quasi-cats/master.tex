\begin{abstract}
	Quasi-categories are simplicial sets which fill inner horns. First introduced by Boardman and Vogt in \cite{BV73}, they have recently become the standard model for $(\infty, 1)$-categories, largely due to the work of Andr\'e Joyal (\cite{joyal1,joyal2}) and Jacob Lurie (\cite{htt, kerodon}). We will introduce the requisite theory of categories and simplicial sets to understand the definition of a quasi-category. We will then explore some of the basic definitions to better understand what an $(\infty ,1)$-category truly is.
\end{abstract}
