\section{Introduction}

In this talk, we will introduce quasi-categories as models for $\infty $-categories. This talk is technically accessible to the average undergraduate student with some basic upper-level training in mathematics, but more realistically the audience member should have had exposure to category theory, and if possible, some algebraic topology and simplicial homotopy.

A good reference for basic category theory is \cite{ml}, and good resources to learn from are \cite{context,lein}. A good reference for the homotopy theory of simplicial sets is \cite{gj}, although we will not need the level of theory they develop. For more accessible and lower level introductions, I recommend \cite{riehlssets}, \cite{fried}, or my own paper \cite{ssets}.
