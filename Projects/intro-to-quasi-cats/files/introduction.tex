\section{Introduction}

In this talk, we will introduce quasi-categories as models for $\infty $-categories. This talk is technically accessible to the advanced undergraduate student with some understanding of category theory, but preferably the reader member should have had exposure to some algebraic topology and simplicial homotopy.

A good reference for basic category theory is \cite{ml}, and good resources to learn from are \cite{context,lein}. For a much more brief overview, the reader can recall my notes \cite{notes} from the last talk I gave to the math club. A good reference for the homotopy theory of simplicial sets is \cite{gj}, although we will not need the level of theory they develop. For more accessible and lower level introductions, I recommend \cite{riehlssets}, \cite{fried}, or my own paper \cite{ssets}, which is currently in preparation.
