\begin{abstract}
	We introduce the $\infty $-derived category $\mathcal{D}_\infty(\mathcal{A} )$ of an abelian category $\mathcal{A} $ as a dg-category, and informally describe the $\infty $-categorical structure by investigating $n$-morphisms and their composition. We define derived and total derived $\infty $-functors, and recover the usual derived category $\mathcal{D} (\mathcal{A} )$ and the usual (total) derived functors via the homotopy category $\hh \mathcal{D} _\infty (\mathcal{A} )$. We conclude by investigating universals in $\mathcal{D} _\infty (\mathcal{A} )$. \cite{weibel}
\end{abstract}
