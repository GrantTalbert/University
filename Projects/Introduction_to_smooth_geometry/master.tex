\begin{abstract}
	These are notes for a talk given to the \href{https://bu-ma.github.io/}{Boston University Mathematics Association} in the Fall 2026 semester. We take a somewhat less rigorous approach to the subject, and focus on breadth and intuition. We introduce manifolds, tangent spaces and the tangent bundle, vector fields the exterior derivative, differential forms, and integration.
\end{abstract}
