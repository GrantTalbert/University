\begin{abstract}
	These notes are synthesized from Mark Hovey's \emph{Model Categories}. I wrote these notes during the summer between my Sophomore and Junior years. My goal was to obtain rigorous understanding of basic model category theory, and to explore Quillen's model structure for $\sSet$, as well as the model structures for $\Ch(\mathcal{A} )$ and $\Top $. Unfortunately I have yet to have the time to formally investigate the specific model structures and the small argument, but I have attained a solid understanding of the basic theory of model categories, including homotopy, Quillen adjunctions and equivalences, and derived functors.
\end{abstract}
