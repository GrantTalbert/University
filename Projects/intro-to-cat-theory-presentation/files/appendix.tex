\section{Glossary of Notation}
\setlength{\parindent}{0em}
$f : X \to Y$: $f$ is a morphism from $X$ to $Y$

$\eta : \mathcal{F}  \Rightarrow \mathcal{G} $: $\eta $ is a natural transformation from $\mathcal{F} $ to $\mathcal{G} $. $\eta _X$ then denotes the component of $\eta $ at $X$, i.e. the morphism $\eta _X : \mathcal{F} (X) \to \mathcal{G} (X)$

$\Hom_{\mathcal{C} }(X, Y) $: class of all morphisms $X\to Y$ in a category $\mathcal{C} $

$\Delta $: the diagonal functor

$\eta $ (in monoids or adjunction): unit in adjunctions and monoids

$\varepsilon $ (in adjunction): counit in adjunctions

$\mu $ (in monoids): multiplication in monoids

$\otimes $: tensor product or monoidal product

$\alpha $: the associator

$\lambda $: the left-unitor

$\rho $: the right-unitor

$\varprojlim \mathcal{F} $: limit of a functor $\mathcal{F} $

$\varinjlim \mathcal{F} $: colimit of a functor $\mathcal{F} $

$\End_{\mathcal{C} }(X) $: morphisms $X\to X$ in a category $\mathcal{C} $

$\Fun(\mathcal{C} ,\mathcal{D} )$: class of all functors $\mathcal{C} \to \mathcal{D} $

$\operatorname{Nat} (\mathcal{F} ,\mathcal{G} )$: class of all natural transformations $ \mathcal{F}  \Rightarrow \mathcal{G} $

$\times $: product

$\amalg$: coproduct

$\oplus $: direct sum

$\cong $: isomorphism

$\Hom_{\mathcal{C} }(X, -) $: the functor $A\mapsto \Hom_{\mathcal{C} }(X, A) $

$h_X$: $\Hom(-, X) $

$X\otimes _R-$: the functor $M\mapsto X\otimes _RM$

$\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{op} } $: the opposite category

$f : X \hookrightarrow Y$: $f$ is an injective function from $X$ to $Y$

$f : X \twoheadrightarrow Y$: $f$ is a surjective function from $X$ to $Y$
