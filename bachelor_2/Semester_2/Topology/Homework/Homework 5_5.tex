\section*{Problem 3}
Let $\mathcal{O} $ be an open cover of $A$. Then
\[
	A=\bigcup_{O\in \mathcal{O} } \left( O \cap A \right) =\left( \bigcup_{O\in \mathcal{O} } O \right) \cap A
.\]
Since $A$ is compact, it admits a finite subcover $\mathcal{O} '$, and thus
\[
	A=\left( \bigcup_{O\in \mathcal{O} '} O \right) \cap A=\bigcup_{O\in \mathcal{O} '} \left( O \cap A \right)
.\]
Therefore, any open cover in the subspace topology admits a finite subcover in the subspace topology.
