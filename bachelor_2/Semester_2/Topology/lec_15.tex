\begin{definition}[Sequential Compactness]\label{dfn:5.3}
	A topological space $X$ is sequentially compact if every sequence $\left\{ x_n \right\}_{n=1} ^{\infty } $ has at least one convergent subsequence in $X$.
\end{definition}
